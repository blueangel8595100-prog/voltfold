Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, email, shipping address, and payment details (processed by our payment provider — we never see or store full card numbers). We also collect basic analytics about how visitors use this site.

How we use it

To fulfill orders, send order updates, respond to support requests, and (only if you opt in) send occasional product emails. We do not sell your personal data.

Third parties

We share data only with services required to run the store: payment processing, shipping carriers, and analytics providers, each bound by their own privacy terms.

Your rights

Email hello@voltfold.com to request a copy or deletion of your data at any time.
