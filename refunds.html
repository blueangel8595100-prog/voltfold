Refund & Shipping Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Shipping

Free tracked shipping within the US. Orders ship within 1–2 business days and arrive in 5–9 business days. You'll receive a tracking number by email.

Returns

30-day no-questions-asked returns. Email hello@voltfold.com with your order number and we'll send a prepaid return label. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving.

Damaged or defective items

If your Trio arrives damaged or stops working within the 1-year warranty, we'll replace it free — no return required in most cases. Just send a photo or short video.
